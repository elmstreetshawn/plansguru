Who's in it: everyone invited, from the moment the invite arrives, before anyone says yes. Opens from the Invite (6) and Plan detail (5).

Friend-of-friend: Priya shows as "Priya · Dee's friend", with no photo. A ＋ badge on her avatar and an ＋ Add friend button next to her name show you can add her. Tap either.

Friend request in 2 steps: pick what you share → review and send, with ← Back to change your picks. Every heading says whose info it is ("What you share with Priya", "Priya shares back"), so first-timers don't mix up the two sides.

Safety: ⋮ → Mute or Leave. You can also remove a friend later, or never add them.

Leaving: you keep what was said up to the moment you left, and see nothing new after that. The message box is replaced with "You left this chat". "Can't make it" works the same way, so the Invite should warn: "You'll leave the plan chat."

After the plan: the chat stays open until everyone has left. Then it becomes read-only history under Past, and you can still send a friend request from it.

Not in the chat: no Guru recommendations or scores. The plan card is pinned at the top instead.

Two ideas, two tabs. A squad is a group you plan with (has a name and emoji, starts a plan). A tag is a word that describes someone (work, gym, neighbors).

For: each list stays short, and the words mean what people expect: WhatsApp has groups, Google Contacts has labels.

Against: two things to learn and build. "Taco crew" could fit either, so people may not know which to make.

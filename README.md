# PlansGuru 🌮✨
### The 100% On-Device, Privacy-First Social Graph & Group Dinner Coordination Engine

---

## 📖 What is PlansGuru?
PlansGuru is a next-generation Android application designed to solve the eternal friction of group planning—starting with the Friday night dinner dilemma. Instead of endless group chat debates or sacrificing privacy to cloud-based reservation platforms, PlansGuru uses an **encrypted local social graph** and an **on-device LLM agent (Guru)** to negotiate and compute the perfect dining consensus for your squad.

---

## 🎯 What is It For?
- **Frictionless Group Coordination:** Automatically calculates recommendations for groups (e.g., Taco Crew, Salad Friends) based on everyone's individual tastes, dietary restrictions, and location zip codes.
- **On-Device Preference Extraction:** Chat naturally with the AI agent to express cuisines you love, dietary restrictions, drive time limits, and loud crowd preferences.
- **Granular Circle Privacy:** Define dining circles (e.g., Close Friends vs. Work Acquaintances) and control precisely which attributes are shared or kept strictly private.
- **Full CRUD Management:** Easily manage people, custom dining spots (Places), dining squads, and preference taxonomies.

---

## 🧘‍♂️ The Ethos & Philosophy Behind PlansGuru

### 1. Absolute Data Sovereignty
In an era where your food preferences, dietary health needs, and social circles are routinely harvested by cloud aggregators, **PlansGuru takes a radical stance: your social graph stays on your phone.** 
- **Zero Cloud Storage:** All profiles, assertions, and preferences are stored locally in an encrypted database (`plansguru_secure.db`) secured by **SQLCipher** and **Android Keystore**.
- **Local-First AI:** Intelligence runs entirely on-device (`LlmInference`), proving that advanced agentic coordination does not require surveillance capitalism.

### 2. Zero-Compromise Group Harmony
Traditional group planning usually results in the "lowest common denominator" compromise where someone eats food they dislike or ignores an allergy. PlansGuru optimizes for **collective joy**:
- **Hard Constraints vs. Soft Preferences:** Severe allergies (peanut, gluten) and strict diets (vegan) act as unyielding hard dealbreakers.
- **Maximized Minimum Satisfaction:** Our multi-variable negotiation engine evaluates group consensus by ensuring that *no single participant* is left compromised (`minSatisfaction` optimization), while maximizing overall group happiness (`avgSatisfaction`).

### 3. Human-Centric Agentic Design
AI should serve as a private concierge, not an intrusive tracker. Guru acts as an encrypted liaison between your friends' devices, negotiating consensus privately and transparently.
